About me

Hello, I’m Julia, a degree-qualified translator and English teacher with more than 14 years of teaching experience. I teach adults one-to-one online, all over the world. Some need English for work, some for an exam or a move abroad, and others simply want to speak with confidence.

I keep learning too. I’m currently preparing for Cambridge Delta Module One, and I follow new developments in language teaching, bringing what works into my lessons.

I use a communicative approach: we work on pronunciation, grammar, speaking, listening and vocabulary together, in English, so you start using new language straight away. We even message in English between lessons. Your individual programme has clear goals and timelines, so you always know where you are and when you’ll reach the next level.

Services

One-to-one online lessons. Choose one area or combine several.

English for the work you actually do: meetings, negotiations, presentations, interviews and everyday emails. We work with material from your own field and build the language your role needs, so you can handle work situations in English with ease.

One-to-one preparation for Cambridge English exams: C1 Advanced (CAE), B2 First (FCE), B1 Preliminary (PET) and A2 Key (KET). You’ll learn how each paper works and how to approach every task, and your general English will improve along the way. We’ll choose your exam together and agree a realistic timeline.

General English for adults on a flexible schedule. A typical pace is about three hours a week, which usually means one level in around nine months. We practise all the skills through topics that interest you, and your materials arrive digitally. In the free trial lesson we’ll check your level and choose a schedule that suits you.

CV help: I can write or translate your CV. Tell me about your experience and the job you’re applying for, and I’ll shape it for that role.

Gift certificates: English lessons as a present for someone close to you. Any amount, for whichever lessons or course they choose. Message me and I’ll help you arrange it.
